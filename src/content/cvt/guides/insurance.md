Insurers use CVT to understand **verified operators** - identity, registration and standing - without treating rumours as underwriting fact.

## What CVT offers insurers

- Verified identity linked to commercial registration 
- Standing built from verified facts (not raw complaints) 
- Access only with **registrant consent** and a declared purpose 
- Governed partner access under agreement - not an open data dump 

CVT prioritises **fair, portable records** in informal mobility. Detailed underwriting data fields, access tiers and technical integration are provided to contracted partners separately.
