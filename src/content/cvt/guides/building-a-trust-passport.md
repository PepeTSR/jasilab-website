A **Trust Passport** is how someone shows that they are verified on CVT - without turning reputation into a hidden score.

## What it includes (plain language)

- **Who you are** on the registry (verified identity and role)
- **Standing** - whether you are in good standing
- **History highlights** - positive verified facts such as tenure, training and insurance where available

It is **not** a safety guarantee and **not** a public dump of every complaint or message about you.

## How people use it

- **Riders and operators** - carry verification beyond one stage or employer
- **Partners** - check standing only with **consent** and for a declared purpose
- **The public** - may see limited verification status on channels CVT supports (for example plate lookup)

## Managing your passport

Verified members manage access through **[My CVT](https://www.cvt.co.ug/en/my-cvt)**. New applicants start at **[Get verified](https://www.cvt.co.ug/en/apply)**.
