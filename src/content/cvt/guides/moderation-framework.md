CVT is built for informal economies where a single rumour can damage a livelihood. **Raw accusations do not change standing.**

## What the public needs to know

- Anyone can report a concern through CVT channels.
- Reports are **reviewed by trained staff** before they can affect a person’s verified standing.
- Unverified or anonymous noise is not treated as fact.
- People can rebuild standing over time - the goal is accountability with room for rehabilitation, not permanent punishment.

## What this means for riders and operators

Your Trust Passport reflects **verified facts** and **moderated outcomes**, not every message someone sends about you.

## What this means for partners

Partner views of standing are based on the same fairness rules. Detailed operational procedures and access rules are shared with partners **under agreement**.
