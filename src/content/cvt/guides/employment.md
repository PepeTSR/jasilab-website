Employers and logistics firms use CVT to check whether an operator is **verified** and in **good standing** - with the person’s **consent**, before hire or fleet onboarding.

## Typical uses

- Hiring boda, taxi or delivery operators 
- Fleet onboarding and periodic re-checks 
- Confirming verification without collecting rumour-based dossiers 

## What you will not get from the public site

CVT does not publish open conduct databases. Partner access is **credentialed**, **purpose-bound** and **consent-gated**. Field-level access and integration steps are shared under agreement.

See [Partners](/partners/) or contact partnerships. Operators manage sharing in **[My CVT](https://www.cvt.co.ug/en/my-cvt)**.
