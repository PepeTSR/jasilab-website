description: "How CVT shares standing with partners - purpose-specific, time-limited and revocable."

CVT does **not** sell open access to people’s records. When a partner needs to see standing, the person must **agree** - for a **specific purpose**, with a **specific organisation**, for a **limited time**.

## Principles

1. **Purpose-specific** - insurance is not the same as employment or finance 
2. **Per organisation** - consent to one partner does not cover another 
3. **Time-limited** - consent expires and can be renewed when needed 
4. **Revocable** - people can withdraw consent; future lookups should fail 
5. **Transparent** - people should be able to see who asked and why (via My CVT)

## What partners get

Partners receive **only what their agreement and consent allow** - never a free browse of the registry. Integration detail and field-level access rules are shared **under partner agreement**.

## What this means for you

Use **[My CVT](https://www.cvt.co.ug/en/my-cvt)** to manage sharing. If you are applying for the first time, start at **[Get verified](https://www.cvt.co.ug/en/apply)**.
